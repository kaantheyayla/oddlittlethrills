About

Danceable, down-tempo music laced in nostalgia and cinematic textures — “like faded VHS tapes of half-remembered dreams.”

Kaan
Istanbul
Mindolé
Arkansas
Based in
Prague

Their debut EP, there was, there wasn’t, is a sensorial journey through simultaneous, layered timelines travelling together yet apart, like parallel trains — somewhere between Boy Harsher and Morcheeba, all ominous pop textures and drum-machine beats.

Eastern-sounding guitars reverberate through steady electronic drums, textured percussion and understated vocals: eerie and comforting, shimmering and solid, cold and hot.
